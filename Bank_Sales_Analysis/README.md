# Project 2: Advanced SQL Project
## Bank Customer Transaction & Risk Analytics

### Business Case
Global Trust Bank (GTB) has over 5 million customers across India. Every day, customers perform deposits, withdrawals, fund transfers, UPI payments, cheque transactions, ATM withdrawals, and online banking transactions. The Analytics Team has been asked to prepare reports for different departments.

### Department Requirements

#### 1. Branch Manager
* Total business of each branch
* Branch-wise deposits
* Branch-wise withdrawals
* Branch-wise average transaction
* Best-performing branch
* Lowest-performing branch

#### 2. Customer Relationship Team
* Premium customers
* Customers making frequent transactions
* High-value customers
* Dormant customers
* Young customers using digital banking
* Senior citizen banking analysis

#### 3. Risk Department
* Transactions above ₹2,00,000
* Multiple high-value transactions
* Cash transactions above RBI limit
* Large withdrawals
* Large deposits
* Suspicious transaction patterns

#### 4. Finance Department
* Total deposits
* Total withdrawals
* Monthly business
* Quarterly business
* Revenue growth
* Average transaction amount

#### 5. Digital Banking Team
* UPI usage
* ATM usage
* Net Banking usage
* Mobile Banking usage
* Digital payment percentage
